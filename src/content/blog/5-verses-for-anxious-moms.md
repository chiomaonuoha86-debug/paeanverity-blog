Some days the noise doesn't stop. The baby's crying, the to-do list is longer than the day, and your mind is running five steps ahead of your body. If you've ever felt your chest tighten in the middle of an ordinary afternoon — you're not alone, and you're not failing.

Scripture doesn't promise the noise will stop. But it does promise Someone steady in the middle of it.

Here are five verses worth returning to, again and again.

## 1. Philippians 4:6-7

*"Do not be anxious about anything, but in every situation, by prayer and petition, with thanksgiving, present your requests to God."*

This isn't a command to feel nothing — it's an invitation to bring the feeling somewhere.

## 2. Psalm 94:19

*"When anxiety was great within me, your consolation brought me joy."*

Notice the order. The anxiety was already great — and comfort still came.

## 3. 1 Peter 5:7

*"Cast all your anxiety on him because he cares for you."*

Casting implies weight — something you're actually carrying. This verse tells you where to put it down.

## 4. Isaiah 41:10

*"So do not fear, for I am with you; do not be dismayed, for I am your God."*

Presence before promise.

## 5. Matthew 11:28

*"Come to me, all you who are weary and burdened, and I will give you rest."*

Not "come once you've figured it out." Just — come.
